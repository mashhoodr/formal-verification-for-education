# Dimension 7: Stage 0 Formal Proof of KC Generation & What the Proof Does *Not* Test

## 1. Purpose: Proving the Generated KCs Before Generating Any Quiz

When applying formal verification to a national curriculum—such as the **National Curriculum of Pakistan (NCP 2022–23)** for **Mathematics** and **English**—the curriculum documents provide **Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs)** across Grades 1–12, not Atomic Knowledge Components (KCs).

If an LLM decomposes a vertical triad of official SLOs $\langle \text{SLO}_{G-1}, \text{SLO}_G, \text{SLO}_{G+1} \rangle$ into candidate Atomic KCs, **Stage 0 Verification (`app/verifier/kc_ontology_kernel.py`)** proves that the generated KC Graph satisfies **6 mathematical ontology & boundary theorems** in Z3 (`SAT`) before Stage 1 ever generates an assessment item.

---

## 2. The 6 Theorems Proven by the Stage 0 Z3 Kernel

Given a Vertical Curriculum Triad across adjacent grades $G-1$ (Prior Floor), $G$ (Target Grade), and $G+1$ (Forbidden Upper Ceiling):

### Theorem 0.1: Prerequisite DAG Acyclicity (`kc_dag_acyclicity`)
Each candidate KC $k_i \in \mathcal{K}$ is assigned a symbolic integer topological rank variable $\text{rank}(k_i) \in [1, |\mathcal{K}|]$. For every directed prerequisite edge $(u \to v) \in E_{\text{prereq}}$:

$$\text{track\_dag\_edge}_{u \to v} \implies \big(\text{rank}(u) < \text{rank}(v)\big)$$

- **What `UNSAT` catches:** Any circular prerequisite loop ($\text{KC}_A \to \text{KC}_B \to \text{KC}_C \to \text{KC}_A$). Z3's minimal `UNSAT Core` isolates the exact cycle of edges.

### Theorem 0.2: Cross-Grade Vertical Monotonicity & Spiral Bridge (`kc_vertical_monotonicity` & `kc_vertical_bridge`)
1. **No Backward Grade Dependencies:** Every prerequisite edge $(u \to v)$ must flow forward or stay within the same grade:
   $$\forall (u \to v) \in E_{\text{prereq}}, \quad \text{grade}(u) \le \text{grade}(v)$$
2. **Connected Spiral Progression:** At least one KC in Target Grade $G$ must have a prerequisite in Grade $G-1$, and at least one KC in Grade $G+1$ must have a prerequisite in Grade $G$:
   $$\sum_{u \in \mathcal{K}_{G-1},\, v \in \mathcal{K}_G} \text{edge}(u, v) \ge 1 \quad \land \quad \sum_{v \in \mathcal{K}_G,\, w \in \mathcal{K}_{G+1}} \text{edge}(v, w) \ge 1$$

### Theorem 0.3: Cross-Grade Envelope Disjointness & Strict Grade Delta (`kc_grade_boundary_disjointness`)
To handle overlapping SLOs across grades (e.g., Pakistan NCP Fractions `M-02-A-32` vs. `M-03-A-24` vs. `M-04-A-23`, or English Reading `E-02-B1-08` vs. `E-03-B2-01` vs. `E-04-B2-01`), each KC is parameterized by a symbolic `(operationId, mechanismComplexityLevel)` signature:
1. **Zero Cross-Grade Boundary Collision:** No KC in Grade $G$ may share the exact same `(operationId, mechanismComplexityLevel)` signature as a KC in Grade $G-1$ or Grade $G+1$:
   $$\forall u \in \mathcal{K}_G,\; \forall v \in \mathcal{K}_{G-1} \cup \mathcal{K}_{G+1}, \quad \neg\big(\text{op}(u) = \text{op}(v) \land \mu(u) = \mu(v)\big)$$
2. **Strict Vertical Complexity Progression:** The maximum mechanism complexity level in Grade $G$ must strictly exceed Grade $G-1$, and remain strictly below Grade $G+1$'s ceiling:
   $$\max_{u \in \mathcal{K}_{G-1}} \mu(u) < \max_{v \in \mathcal{K}_G} \mu(v) < \max_{w \in \mathcal{K}_{G+1}} \mu(w)$$

### Theorem 0.4: Cognitive Atomicity & Working Memory Cap (`kc_atomicity_bounds`)
Every candidate KC $k_i$ must represent a single indivisible mental rule bounded by the developmental stage's working memory limit:

$$\forall k_i \in \mathcal{K}, \quad \text{verbCount}(k_i) = 1 \;\land\; \text{observableVars}(k_i) \le W_{\text{cap}}(\text{grade}(k_i))$$

- **What `UNSAT` catches:** Composite "blob" KCs generated by LLMs (e.g., *"Identify equivalent fractions and simplify fractions to lowest terms"* bundled into a single node with `verbCount = 2`).

### Theorem 0.5: Diagnostic Misconception Sufficiency (`kc_misconception_sufficiency`)
To guarantee that Stage 1 can construct diagnostic MCQs with bijective misconception distractors, every KC in Grade $g$ must define at least $M_{\min}(g)$ distinct student misconceptions ($M_{\min} = 2$ for Grades 1–2; $M_{\min} = 3$ for Grades 3–12):

$$\forall k_i \in \mathcal{K}, \quad |\text{Distinct}(\mathcal{M}_{k_i})| \ge M_{\min}(\text{grade}(k_i))$$

### Theorem 0.6: Bipartite Official SLO Clause Coverage & Zero Hallucinated Orphans (`slo_clause_coverage`)
Let $C_{\text{SLO}}(g)$ be the set of official Pakistan NCP curriculum clauses for Grade $g$:
1. **Complete Clause Coverage:** Every official SLO clause $c \in C_{\text{SLO}}(g)$ is covered by at least one KC in Grade $g$:
   $$\forall c \in C_{\text{SLO}}(g), \quad \sum_{k \in \mathcal{K}_g} \text{covers}(k, c) \ge 1$$
2. **Zero Hallucinated / Orphan KCs:** Every generated KC in Grade $g$ must map to at least one official SLO clause in $C_{\text{SLO}}(g)$:
   $$\forall k \in \mathcal{K}_g, \quad \sum_{c \in C_{\text{SLO}}(g)} \text{covers}(k, c) \ge 1$$

## 3. Epistemic Boundaries: What Is *NOT* Tested by the Stage 0 Proof?

A rigorous formal verification system must explicitly state **what lies outside its proof boundary**. Below are the **6 properties that Stage 0 Z3 verification does *not* prove**, why they cannot be proven purely inside an SMT solver, and how the broader system addresses them:

| Untested Dimension (Outside Z3 Stage 0) | Why Z3 Cannot Prove It Alone | Example of What Could Slip Through Without External Checks | Mitigation Layer in Our Architecture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Semantic Truth of Coverage Claims ("The Label-Mismatch Illusion")** | Z3 operates over symbolic IDs and arithmetic invariants. If a KC claims `coversSloClauses: ["M-03-A-21.c1"]`, Z3 proves the bipartite graph edge exists, not that the English prose of the KC matches the English prose of the SLO. | A KC titled *"Measure length in centimeters"* is tagged with `coversSloClauses: ["M-03-A-21.c1"]` (Equivalent Fractions). Z3 sees `coverage >= 1` (`SAT`). | **Pre-Proof Lexical Domain Anchor Check:** Deterministic keyword/stem overlap check between the official SLO clause text and the KC description before asserting `covers(k, c) = 1`. |
| **2. Empirical Classroom Reality of Misconceptions** | Z3 proves that a KC has $\ge 3$ *syntactically distinct* misconception IDs (`Distinct(m1, m2, m3)`). It cannot prove that real children in Grade 3 actually hold misconception `m3` in the real world. | An LLM invents a mathematically arbitrary error rule (*"Student cubes the denominator and subtracts 7"*) that no human child ever makes. | **Curated Misconception Ontology / Psychometric Field Calibration:** Grounding $\mathcal{M}_k$ in published math/literacy error taxonomies and validating distractor selection rates ($>5\%$ pick rate) on real student responses. |
| **3. Numerical Non-Degeneracy of Misconceptions Across All Inputs** | Two misconceptions $m_1$ and $m_2$ can be conceptually distinct as rules in Stage 0, yet collide to the **exact same number** for specific inputs in Stage 1! | For fraction $\frac{2}{2}$, *"adding 2 to top and bottom"* ($\frac{4}{4}$) and *"multiplying top and bottom by 2"* ($\frac{4}{4}$) produce the exact same numerical answer! | **Stage 1 Instance-Level Distractor Distinctness Proof:** When concrete numbers are chosen for an MCQ in Stage 1, Z3 proves `Distinct(val(key), val(d1), val(d2), val(d3))`. |
| **4. Empirical Cognitive Transfer Along Prerequisite Edges** | Z3 proves the prerequisite graph is a valid, acyclic, grade-monotonic DAG. It cannot prove causal learning transfer in human working memory. | Edge `KC-A -> KC-B` is acyclic and same-grade (`SAT`), but teaching `KC-A` does not empirically improve student pass rates on `KC-B`. | **Post-Assessment Psychometric Audit (DINA / BKT):** Checking empirical conditional probability $P(\text{Pass } B \mid \text{Fail } A) \approx g_B$ across classroom cohorts. |
| **5. Upstream SLO Sentence Segmentation Completeness** | Z3 proves coverage of the clause list $C_{\text{SLO}}$ passed into Stage 0. If an upstream parser splits a 3-part SLO sentence into only 2 clauses, the 3rd part is invisible to Z3. | Official SLO says *"Compare, order, and round decimals"* but upstream parser only registers `c1: compare` and `c2: order`, omitting `round`. | **Deterministic Conjunction & Verb-Phrase Splitter:** Auditing SLO clause extraction against comma/conjunction verb counts in the raw NCP PDF text. |
| **6. Honesty of Upstream KC Annotations** | Theorems 0.3–0.5 check integer labels (`verbCount`, `mechanismComplexity`, `observableVariablesCount`) supplied by the KC generator. Z3 proves the labels satisfy the invariants, not that they were assigned truthfully. | An LLM "repairs" a Grade 3 / Grade 4 collision by renumbering `mechanismComplexity` from 4 to 2 without changing the KC's content. | **Deterministic label derivation + repair diffing:** count verbs from the KC title, derive envelopes from explicit NCP ranges (e.g. denominators), and flag repairs that only change labels. |

## 4. Proof Strength: Which Theorems Are Genuinely Symbolic?

Being honest about this matters, given the earlier question of [what makes a proof stronger than an `if` statement](../../formal-verification-vs-if-else.md). The six theorems aren't equally strong:

| Theorem | Proof character | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0.1 DAG Acyclicity** | **Genuinely symbolic (existential)** | Z3 must *find* an assignment of `rank` variables satisfying every edge simultaneously. No single edge check reveals a cycle; only the global system does. The `UNSAT Core` returns the minimal cycle. |
| **0.2A Vertical Monotonicity** | Ground-fact check | Each edge compares two known grade integers. Equivalent to a loop of `if` statements. |
| **0.2B Spiral Bridge** | Ground-fact check (aggregate) | Edge counts are computed in Python and bound as facts. |
| **0.3A Boundary Disjointness** | Ground-fact check | Signatures are known integers; Z3 evaluates `Not(op == op ∧ mech == mech)`. |
| **0.3B Complexity Progression** | Ground-fact check (aggregate) | Per-grade max complexity is computed and bound as facts. |
| **0.4 / 0.5 Atomicity & Misconceptions** | Ground-fact check | Counts are bound as facts. |
| **0.6 Clause Coverage / No Orphans** | Ground-fact check (aggregate) | Coverage counts are computed and bound as facts. |

**What Z3 still adds for the ground-fact theorems:** one uniform audit artifact (the SMT-LIB2 script), the joint `UNSAT Core` showing every violated theorem at once, and a SHA-256 certificate over the verified graph. **What it does not add:** search. They become genuinely symbolic once the KC generator's choices are left as free variables (e.g. letting Z3 *choose* `mechanismComplexity` values or prerequisite edges subject to these constraints). That's the natural next step for `z3.Optimize`-driven repair.
